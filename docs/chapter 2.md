INTRODUCTION TO SURVEY


In SpeakInsights, surveys act as a crucial mechanism to gather structured feedback from meeting participants regarding meeting effectiveness, clarity, engagement, and action items. By enabling systematic data collection, surveys allow organizers and administrators to understand participant experiences, measure satisfaction, and identify areas for improvement in both meeting management and AI-driven summaries..

The survey feature is implemented using a React + FastAPI + PostgreSQL stack, ensuring a secure, responsive, and scalable platform. Participants can submit feedback conveniently from any device immediately after a meeting. All responses are stored in a centralized database and can be analyzed in real-time to generate actionable insights, improve AI summarization quality, and refine meeting workflows.

By incorporating surveys, SpeakInsights empowers participants to actively contribute 
to meeting quality, fosters transparency in organizational communication, and enhances
 post-meeting decision-making, ultimately creating a more effective, engaging, 
and productive collaboration environment.


	

WHY SURVEY?


Surveys in SpeakInsights are used to collect structured and actionable feedback from meeting participants in an organized and efficient manner. Unlike informal comments or verbal feedback, surveys provide quantifiable data that can be analyzed to improve both the technical and human aspects of meetings.

                  Key reasons for using surveys include:
Understanding Participant Experience: Surveys reveal how participants perceive meeting clarity, pace, engagement, and usefulness of AI summaries.
Improving Meeting Quality: Feedback helps organizers refine meeting structure, AI transcription, summarization accuracy, and overall user experience.
Transparency and Accountability: Surveys give participants a voice, ensuring that their feedback informs decision-making and meeting improvements.
Data-Driven Planning: Collected responses can be analyzed to identify patterns, highlight recurring issues, and guide enhancements to features or processes.
Engagement and Participation: Encouraging post-meeting feedback increases participant involvement and fosters a culture of continuous improvement.
